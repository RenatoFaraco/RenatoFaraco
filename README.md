# Renato Faraco

Juiz de Fora, Brazil (UTC-3) 

## About Me

Electrical Engineer (MSc, PhD candidate at the Federal University of Juiz de Fora - UFJF) with 8+ years of experience building measurement and embedded systems end to end: sensor, firmware, host tooling, and data analysis. I have shipped embedded and Rust software into real industrial deployments, including a supervisory and acquisition system for a fiber-optic monitoring network at a hydroelectric plant.

My daily work focuses on RISC-V (ESP32-C3, WCH CH32V) and ARM (Cortex-M, Raspberry Pi / embedded Linux) architectures across bare-metal and FreeRTOS/ESP-IDF environments, emphasizing clean hardware abstraction layers, host-side unit testing, and CI. I am also an open-source author (MIT-licensed embedded logging framework), with 15 peer-reviewed journal papers (70+ citations) and 7 patent filings in sensing and instrumentation
